# Sources and reuse

The primary exposition is [Chapter V, Connected Spaces](https://www.math.wustl.edu/~freiwald/topology/Ch5.pdf#page=19), from the topology notes hosted at Washington University under Freiwald's directory. Definition 5.7 defines a continuum as a compact connected Hausdorff space. Lemma 5.9 supplies the shrinking construction, and Theorem 5.10 gives Sierpiński's closed-partition theorem. These are on printed pages 231 through 233. The formalization imposes neither metrizability nor local connectedness.

The local Boundary Bumping section is reused from [Arthur742Ramos/boundary-bumping-lean at commit `58831416a12f73312ac01cdaec19c060a6d4b142`](https://github.com/Arthur742Ramos/boundary-bumping-lean/tree/58831416a12f73312ac01cdaec19c060a6d4b142), under Apache 2.0. Its credited authors are Arthur Freitas Ramos, David Barros Hulak, and Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz. A previously verified local copy supplied the proof, and the pinned public source rendering was retrieved for comparison. Only the module documentation is adapted. The private review archive records the reused theorem bodies and their byte/token comparison. This section is ordinary local Lean code and adds no package dependency.

Mathlib supplies compactness, normal separation, components, subtype topology, equalizer closedness, and Cantor intersection nonemptiness. The exact revision is `065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55`. A complete search of the pinned repository's 9,090 Lean files found no equivalent target; the private review archive retains the complete inventory and candidate assessment. This records the coverage of that source search, without claiming worldwide formalization priority.

The [Isabelle/HOL `locally_connected_not_countable_closed_union`](https://github.com/isabelle-prover/mirror-isabelle/blob/master/src/HOL/Analysis/Urysohn.thy) result was inspected in the current public Urysohn source. It requires local connectedness in addition to connectedness and either complete metrizability or locally compact Hausdorffness. Its pieces must be nonempty. It therefore does not supply this task's full compact Hausdorff statement. No Isabelle proof code is reused here.

The countable-type wrapper pads an injective enumeration with empty pieces. The continuous-choice corollary applies the partition theorem to closed equalizers. These are direct consequences of the main theorem. Independent AI review approved the mathematical scope and local proof checks without corrections. The formalization claims no new mathematics, source-author endorsement, or completed human mathematical review.

Human direction selected the target, scope, proof route, and resource constraints. Lean code and verification scripts were developed with AI assistance. Reader-facing prose was edited using the installed Humanizer skill while preserving the mathematical scope and verification limitations.
